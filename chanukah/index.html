    Bring the light home

    The heart of Chanukah is at home. The main mitzvah of Chanukah is lighting the menorah in your own home, each night of the holiday, where your family and your neighbors can see it.

    Don’t have a menorah, or need candles? Request one from the Chabad Jewish Center and we’ll get it to you, with everything you need for all eight nights.
